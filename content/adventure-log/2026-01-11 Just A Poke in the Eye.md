## Summary

Picking up in the magical, thrumming depths of [[Wave Echo Cave]]—shortly after discovering the body of [[Tharden]] Rockseeker, receiving a warning from the celestial ally [[Dracalis]] about [[Gabriel]]'s reality-threatening machinations, and fighting off undead—the party found themselves face-to-face with a volatile fungal cavern. With [[Alinar]]'s player absent (and his character run on reliable, Eldritch-Blasting autopilot), [[Jae]] Paire, [[Aeolus]], and [[Melody]] rolled for initiative as lethal gaspore and violet fungi closed in. Jae Paire immediately charged forward and cleaved into a gaspore fungus with his battleaxe, Hew, dealing maximum damage against the plant creature. However, the victory was short-lived as the fungus violently ruptured upon death, showering the area in toxic spores that left Alinar poisoned and at risk of sprouting deadly mushrooms from his own body within hours.

Thinking quickly, the party adjusted their tactics to neutralize the fungal threat from afar. Alinar used Repelling Blast to shove and destroy a second gaspore fungus outside of detonation range, while Melody and Aeolus pelted the sluggish violet fungi with Starry Wisp and Ray of Frost. After finishing off the remaining hazards and curing Alinar's lethal infection with Keoghtom's Ointment, the party surveyed the glowing cavern. They deduced that the undisturbed fungal network meant their rival, [[Nezznar the Spider|The Spider]], had not yet passed this way or located the [[Forge of Souls]]. Pushing northward, they entered a breathtaking cavern whose glittering mineral ceiling mimicked a starry night sky, though its floor was littered with dozens of crushed skeletons and blackened masonry from a centuries-old wizard battle.

Sensing an uneasy aura to the southwest and potent magic to the north, Aeolus guided the party toward the northern double doors, which were scorched and melted shut. Calling upon "the power of friendship" (along with Guidance, Bardic Inspiration, and a Fortune Point), Jae Paire forced the heavy iron doors open to reveal the legendary Forge of Souls: a ruined workshop housing an untouched stone pedestal and a brazier of crackling green flame. Hovering behind it was a four-eyestalked Spectator. In a hilarious role reversal, the usually cynical Aeolus attempted to negotiate with the aberration while Jae Paire braced for violence. The Spectator politely informed them that while it existed to protect the forge, its orders strictly included guarding the door they had just forced open—initiating a chaotic boss battle.

The Spectator proved to be a formidable magical threat, deflecting spells with its arcane mirror and barraging the party with eye rays that paralyzed Alinar, frightened Aeolus, and afflicted Melody with confusion. Fueled by an upcast Bless from Jae Paire, the party chipped away at the aberration with cantrips, Shatter, and a musical, conductor-style Sacred Flame. In the climax of the fight, a confused Melody stumbled in circles critiquing her companions' outfits before closing her eyes, channeling her late companion Drakka, and firing a True Strike crossbow bolt straight through the Spectator's central pupil. With the guardian slain, the party unearthed two potent artifacts from a nearby coal kiln—the blessed mace Lightbringer and a +2 Dragon Guard Breastplate—and prepared to rest before confronting whatever else lurks in the mines.

## Scenes

## Spore Losers in the Fungal Cavern

### The party battles exploding gaspore fungi and creeping violet fungi while discovering the path to the Forge is undisturbed.

- Returning from the previous cliffhanger in the fungal cavern, the party rolled initiative; Jae Paire rolled a Natural 20 (with advantage from his Sentinel Shield) after the GM cleaned up duplicate tracker entries.
- Jae Paire rushed a gaspore fungus and struck it with Hew (18 to hit), dealing a maximum 12 slashing damage due to the axe's plant-slaying properties.
- The gaspore fungus exploded on death, forcing DC 10 Constitution saves across the party; Jae Paire succeeded, Aeolus was completely immune to poison and the poisoned condition via his Periapt, and an absent Alinar failed, taking 3d6 poison damage and contracting a lethal 5-hour spore infection.
- Jae Paire rolled a 24 on a Medicine check, diagnosing that anyone afflicted by the gaspore poison would die and sprout tiny fungi unless cured.
- Controlled by the GM, Alinar fired two Eldritch Blasts with Repelling Blast at a second gaspore fungus, pushing it 20 feet back and detonating it safely out of range.
- Aeolus cast Ray of Frost at a sluggish violet fungus and retreated 15 feet while sending his owl familiar, Ozy, to fly high above danger.
- Melody hit a fungus with Starry Wisp for 5 damage as the remaining violet fungi dashed toward the party.
- Jae Paire rolled his second Natural 1 of the session with his new brass dice, missing a melee swing and declining to spend a Fortune Point.
- Alinar eliminated one rear fungus with Eldritch Blast (6 damage)—noting it didn't explode when killed that way—and bloodied another for 10 damage.
- After Aeolus missed with a Ray of Frost, Melody killed a violet fungus with Starry Wisp (15 to hit).
- The last violet fungus missed Jae Paire with a 10-foot reach attack; Jae Paire rolled a 22 Survival check to confirm violet fungi aren't poisonous and cleaved it for maximum plant damage (12), leaving it barely alive for Alinar to finish off with a 7-damage Eldritch Blast.
- Out of combat, Aeolus rolled a 10 on Nature and decided not to harvest the questionable mushroom corpses.
- Jae Paire applied Keoghtom's Ointment to Alinar, curing his poisoned condition and restoring hit points.
- Aeolus and Melody surveyed the cavern and deduced that the intact fungal traps meant the Black Spider had not come this way yet; Aeolus proposed having Ozy scout ahead for spores.

### Beneath the Glittering Escarpment

### The adventurers traverse a starry, skeleton-strewn cavern and muscle their way through the fused doors of the Forge of Souls.

- Moving north, the party entered a massive cavern where glittering ceiling minerals resembled a night sky—inspiring Melody to brainstorm theatrical set designs—and crushed skeletons lay beneath ancient debris.
- Jae Paire rolled a 13 on Survival, confirming no natural hazards remained and recognizing the scorched walls and rubble as the aftermath of a centuries-old wizard battle over the mines.
- Aeolus rolled a 25 on Arcana, detecting an uneasy magical aura to the southwest (suspected to be the Spider) and an overwhelmingly strong magical aura through the fused iron double doors to the north.
- Aeolus used Wand of Secrets to check for hidden passages (finding none) and used Message to coordinate moving the party north.
- Evaluating the heavy, melted iron doors to the north, the party debated using Knock or Shatter before Aeolus cast Guidance on Jae Paire to force them open (DC 15 Athletics).
- Jae Paire rolled an 8 on the die, failing even with Guidance and a retroactively applied d8 Bardic Inspiration from Melody (totaling 13).
- Refusing to let the moment fail, Jae Paire spent a Fortune Point to reroll, channeled the "power of friendship" and a bit of imaginary heckling from Alinar, succeeded on the check, and wrenched the groaning iron doors open.
- Inside the ruined workshop stood an untouched stone pedestal bearing a small brazier of eerie green flame, guarded by a floating Spectator that telepathically welcomed Jae Paire to the Forge of Souls.

### Eye of the Spectator

### A failed diplomatic pitch leads to a chaotic shootout against the Forge's multi-eyed guardian.

- Aeolus rolled an 11 on Arcana to recall that Spectators are conjured guardians bound to a specific task, and attempted to persuade the creature that the party were allies here to protect the Forge from a malevolent drow.
- After briefly debating whether to spend Fortune Points on a Persuasion check (and backing out upon realizing it would hand the GM those Fortune Points), the Spectator stated its orders included guarding the door they just broke, triggering initiative.
- Jae Paire rolled a Natural 1 despite having advantage on initiative; Alinar went first, blasting the Spectator twice with Eldritch Blast (dirty 20 and a hit) for 24 total damage and pushing it back.
- Aeolus moved into the room and hit the Spectator with Poison Spray for 8 poison damage.
- Melody cast Shatter (the Spectator passed its save for 8 half-damage, blasting debris off the shelves behind it) and rolled a successful DC 13 Dexterity save in her "4-inch dodging heels" when the Spectator's arcane mirror deflected energy back at her.
- The Spectator fired three eye rays: Aeolus passed a DC 13 Con save for half damage, Jae Paire rolled a 17 total on a DC 13 Con save to resist paralysis, and Melody failed a DC 13 Wisdom save against 12 psychic damage before spending her Heroic Inspiration to reroll a dirty 20 and negate the effect.
- The Spectator flew 15 feet horizontally and 15 feet into the air.
- Jae Paire upcast Bless at 2nd level to buff all four party members, used his Sentinel Shield advantage to roll a Natural 20 on Perception—spotting strange objects buried in the coals of a nearby kiln—and ended his turn.
- Alinar moved in and hit with Eldritch Blast (29 to hit) for 14 damage, bloodying the Spectator before its innate mechanics healed it slightly.
- Aeolus dealt 13 damage with a cantrip to bloody it again, and Melody cast a second Shatter for 7 damage.
- On its next turn, the Spectator unleashed another volley: Jae Paire and Alinar passed DC 13 Wisdom saves (aided by Bless), but a second ray paralyzed Alinar.
- Jae Paire cast Toll the Dead (accidentally targeting the whole party in Foundry before fixing it), which the Spectator saved against; Jae Paire then passed a DC 13 Dexterity save ("Limber Larry") to dodge the Spectator's spell reflection.
- Alinar used his turn to repeat his save and broke free of paralysis.
- Aeolus blasted the Spectator for 14 damage, leaving it in critical condition, and Melody followed up with Starry Wisp for 4 damage.
- Desperate, the Spectator fired three more rays: Aeolus took 11 necrotic damage and 7 psychic damage (becoming Frightened), while Melody failed a DC 13 Wisdom save, taking 12 psychic damage and becoming Confused.
- Out of melee reach, Jae Paire cast Sacred Flame like an orchestra conductor; the Spectator failed its DC 15 Dexterity save, leaving it clinging to life.
- After Alinar landed one more hit and a frightened Aeolus missed with disadvantage, a confused Melody rolled a 2 on a d4 to determine her random target—luckily landing on the Spectator.
- Channeling Drakka and firing her True Strike crossbow (+10 to hit, 25 total), Melody dealt 11 damage and pierced the Spectator's central eye to kill it.

### Treasures in the Soot

### The party uncovers legendary dwarven armaments in the kiln coals and debates resting in the Forge.

- Investigating the kiln he spotted earlier, Jae Paire rolled a Natural 1 (a total of 0), tripping face-first into the coals and emerging covered in soot before Melody quickly cleaned him with Prestidigitation.
- Aeolus and Melody investigated the kiln (both rolling 15s with Guidance) and pulled out a dwarven-crafted mace and a breastplate.
- As Melody held the mace, it began to glow alongside Lathander's sigil on Alinar's forehead; Jae Paire rolled a 25 on Religion to identify it as Lightbringer, a +2 mace that deals bonus radiant damage.
- Aeolus rolled Arcana to identify the armor as a +2 Breastplate with protective properties against dragons.
- Aeolus rolled a Natural 20 to assess whether the room was safe enough to rest and immediately laid his head down on the Spectator's squished eyeball like a pillow to conquer his earlier fear.
- At the end of the session, the party voted Jae Paire as Most Valuable Character, awarding him the campaign's first Loric Inspiration (a free d6 usable on any roll).

## NPCs

## The Spectator

A spherical, four-eyestalked aberration conjured centuries ago to guard the Forge of Souls. Though initially willing to converse telepathically and hear out Aeolus's pitch for cooperation, its strict orders to guard the workshop door forced it into combat. It was ultimately slain by a confused crossbow shot from Melody straight through its central pupil, and its corpse was subsequently used as a pillow by Aeolus.

## Thardin & Nundro Rockseeker

Dwarven brothers mentioned during the session recap. Thardin's body was previously discovered by the party inside Wave Echo Cave, while clues indicated that Nundro may still be alive deeper within the complex.

## Dracalis

Alinar's ally on the celestial plane, mentioned in the recap as having warned Alinar that Gabriel's machinations threaten the fabric of reality and advising the party to head eastward toward the Forge.

## The Black Spider

The malevolent drow antagonist seeking control of Wave Echo Cave and the Forge of Souls. Based on the undisturbed fungal cavern and Aeolus's Arcana check, the party believes the Spider has not yet reached the Forge, though an uneasy aura lingers to the southwest.

## Drakka

A former companion whose memory and crossbow remain with Melody. During the final shot against the Spectator, Melody felt both Drakka's hand and Drakka's father's hand steadying her aim.

## Locations

## Fungal Cavern

A cavern east of the ravine filled with dim, bioluminescent mushrooms, explosive gaspore fungi, and slow-moving violet fungi. The party deduced that the residual, unused magic of the caves likely mutated the fungi into violent hazards, and its undisturbed state proved the Black Spider had not passed through.

## Starry Escarpment Cavern

A massive subterranean chamber north of the fungal cave whose mineral-flecked ceiling glitters like a night sky under darkvision. Divided by a stone escarpment and stairs, it houses two scorched human-proportioned masonry structures and dozens of skeletons crushed during an ancient wizard battle.

## The Forge of Souls

The legendary northern workshop where magical weapons and armor were once inscribed and imbued with power during Wave Echo Cave's heyday. Though the surrounding workshop was ruined by ancient spellfire, the central stone pedestal and its brazier of crackling green flame remain completely intact, radiating the pulsing magic felt throughout the entire cavern system.

# Items

## Hew

Jae Paire's battleaxe, which grants a +1 bonus and deals maximum slashing damage (12 total on a 1d8+4) when striking plant creatures or wooden objects. Jae Paire used it to obliterate a gaspore fungus and heavily damage a violet fungus.

## Keoghtom's Ointment

A jar of restorative salve carrying five doses. Jae Paire expended one dose after the fungal combat to cure Alinar's lethal gaspore poison and restore his hit points without burning a spell slot.

## Periapt of Proof Against Poison

Worn by Aeolus, rendering him completely immune to poison damage and the poisoned condition when the gaspore fungus exploded in his face.

## Wand of Secrets

Used by Aeolus in the starry cavern to scan for secret doors or traps to bypass the melted iron doors, though none were present.

## Sentinel Shield

Carried by Jae Paire, granting him advantage on initiative rolls (yielding both a Nat 20 and a Nat 1 this session) and Perception checks (yielding a Nat 20 to spot the buried relics in the coal kiln).

## True Strike Crossbow

Drakka's former crossbow, now wielded by Melody. It allows her to cast True Strike to attack using her Charisma modifier (+10 to hit) with bonus cantrip damage (1d6), which she used while confused to deal 11 damage and kill the Spectator.

## Lightbringer

A legendary dwarven-crafted +2 mace pulled from the Forge's kiln by Melody that deals extra radiant damage. Holding it caused the mace and Lathander's sigil on Alinar's forehead to glow in unison.

## +2 Dragon Guard Breastplate

A magical +2 breastplate pulled from the Forge's kiln by Aeolus that carries protective enchantments against dragons.

Alinar's signature cantrip, paired with Repelling Blast to push targets 10 feet on a hit. Used on autopilot throughout the session to safely detonate a gaspore fungus out of range, finish off two violet fungi, and hammer the Spectator for heavy damage.

## Ray of Frost

Cast by Aeolus against the violet fungi and the Spectator, dealing cold damage alongside several comical misses.

Melody's go-to ranged spell attack, used to destroy a violet fungus and chip away at the Spectator.

## Sacred Flame

Cast for the first time by Jae Paire like a symphony conductor emphasizing the downbeat; the Spectator failed its DC 15 Dexterity save and took radiant damage.

## Prestidigitation

Used immediately by Melody to clean the soot off Jae Paire's face after he face-planted into the coal kiln.
