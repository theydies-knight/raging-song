- **Gundren Rockseeker and the Expedition to Phandalin:** Alongside his brothers, Gundren rediscovered the lost entrance to Wave Echo Cave and the legendary [[Forge of Souls]]. While traveling along the Triboar Trail with mining supplies and his partner, [[Sildar Hallwinter]], Gundren was ambushed by Cragmaw goblins and abducted to Cragmaw Castle on orders from the Spider. After being killed by the dire warg Snarl's psychic howl during the castle raid, Gundren was swiftly **revivified by Alinar's divine magic**. He safely returned to Phandalin for an emotional reunion with Sildar, pledging the eternal alliance, shelter, and hospitality of the Rockseeker clan to the party.
- **Tharden Rockseeker's Tragic Demise:** Venturing ahead into the Sword Mountains to explore [[Wave Echo Cave]], Tharden established an initial campsite at the cavern’s threshold. He was ambushed and **slain by [[Nezznar]] the Spider's forces**, left behind amidst ransacked bedrolls at the rim of the chasm. When the adventurers discovered his body, Jae Pierre cast _Speak with Dead_ to interrogate the corpse; Tharden provided crucial tactical warnings about the drow wizard and bugbears, leaving his _Cloak of Protection_ and an irreverent, heartfelt farewell declaring Gundren a "butthead" and Nundro an "idiot".
- **Nundro Rockseeker's Captivity and Retribution:** Captured at the entrance campsite when Tharden fell, Nundro was dragged into the subterranean depths and interrogated daily by Nezznar. The party discovered him bound and unconscious at the base of a dwarven monument in the Temple of Dumathoin, where Aeolus revived him with restorative healing. Upon learning that Tharden had been murdered, a grieving Nundro invoked Dumathoin's name and **executed the defeated Spider with a mining pick to the skull**, surviving to help reclaim the mines for his family.
