## Summary

Recovering from their battle with the spectator, the party stood before the glowing green beacon of the [[Forge of Souls]] inside [[Wave Echo Cave]]. Realizing that taking a full night's rest might allow [[Nezznar, the Spider|The Spider]] to ambush them, [[Aeolus Stormcaller|Aeolus]] and [[Jae Paire]] deduced that a brief respite was the wiser tactical choice. Examining the arcane brazier, the party learned that while its magic only functioned when attached to its specific podium, they could safely stow the artifact away to keep it out of enemy hands. 

Before packing the Forge, the magical breastplate, and the mace *[[Lightbringer]]* into [[Alinar Dormitor|Alinar]]'s *Bag of Holding*, the adventurers bathed their mundane daggers and Alinar's severed spider-leg club in the green flames, imbuing them with temporary magical potency.

Drawn by a lingering, supernatural sense of dread, Aeolus convinced the group to investigate a southern chamber before hunting down the Spider. Jae Paire forced the brittle door open, came face-to-face with a shrieking undead apparition, and immediately slammed the door shut in its face. Despite Jae Paire's vehement arguments that they should simply walk away, [[Melody Sparkleheart|Melody]] opened the door to fire a crossbow bolt, kicking off a chaotic skirmish against the wraith of Mormesk. 

Jae Paire repeatedly attempted to use *Sear Undead*, failing three times in a row against the stubborn spirit and prompting him to try abandoning the room mid-fight while Alinar mocked him for being a cleric who was afraid of ghosts. Ultimately, Alinar blasted the wraith into oblivion with *Eldritch Blast*, lifting the oppressive aura from the cave and allowing the party to loot a scorched chest and Mormesk's old journals.

Pressing northward, the party followed Jae Paire's "always go right" philosophy and discovered the underground lake whose rhythmic, booming waves gave Wave Echo Cave its name. Overcome with excitement at the sight of water, Aeolus the sea druid immediately dove in without checking for danger—and swam straight into a giant freshwater squid. 

Desperate to avoid wasting resources on local wildlife, Jae Paire tossed five rations into the water to chum it while Aeolus cast *Speak with Animals* to apologize for the intrusion. The squid, entirely uninterested in diplomacy, bit Aeolus and dragged a heavily armored Jae Paire into the water with its tentacles. Abandoning peace talks, Aeolus turned the surrounding water into a caustic whirlpool with *Wrath of the Sea*, Melody forced the beast to release the cleric with a *Command* spell, and Alinar fried the cephalopod with *Witch Bolt* and *Eldritch Blast*.

Continuing deeper into the caverns, the party spotted two bugbears guarding the passage where Gundren's brother, Nundro, was likely being held. Pressured by his companions to try diplomacy one more time, Alinar marched up to the guards and unleashed all of his pent-up rage regarding Jae Paire's antics, threatening to murder the bugbears and their entire families if they didn't hand over the dwarf immediately. The terrifying tirade worked flawlessly, sending the bugbears fleeing without a single blow struck. 

Moments later, the party finally cornered the drow mastermind known as [[Nezznar, the Spider|The Spider]]. After a brief, bizarre parley where Jae Paire accused the villain of torturing children and mocked his lack of eight limbs, Aeolus abruptly ended the conversation by casting *Silence* directly over the Spider, plunging the cavern into a quiet, high-stakes battle.

## Scenes

### Licking Wounds at the Forge

***The party rests by the Forge of Souls, debates their next move, and dips their mundane weapons into the green arcane fire.***

- Standing in the chamber of the Forge of Souls after defeating the spectator, the party debated taking a long or short rest.
- Aeolus and Jae Paire both rolled a dirty 20 on Insight checks (with Aeolus using *Guidance*), deducing that a long rest would allow the Spider to get the drop on them.
- The GM granted the partyArcana checks to understand the Forge; Jae Paire rolled a Natural 20, Aeolus rolled a 29, and Alinar rolled a 23.
- They learned the Forge of Souls could be safely picked up and stowed in their *Bag of Holding* to keep it away from the Spider, though its magic only functions while attached to this specific podium.
- Before packing the Forge, the mace *Lightbringer*, and the magical breastplate into the bag, Alinar, Aeolus, and Melody dipped their mundane daggers—and Alinar's severed spider-leg club—into the green flames, granting them a temporary +1 bonus until their next long rest.
- Alinar cast *Cure Wounds* to heal Aeolus and Melody for 28 hit points each before the party took a one-hour short rest, during which the GM generously allowed everyone to restore one spell slot of each level.

### Slamming the Door on Mormesk

***Investigating a dreadful presence to the south, the party battles the wraith of the cave's last wizard while Jae Paire tries to abandon the fight.***

- Aeolus felt a lingering sense of dread coming from a southern door, and the party decided to clear it out.
- Jae Paire pressed his ear to the cold, brittle door, rolling an 8 on Perception and learning only that it was indeed a door.
- After failing a gingerly Sleight of Hand check, Jae Paire forced the door open with Athletics, saw a shrieking undead spirit inside, and immediately slammed the door shut, earning a Fortune Point from the GM for the hilarious reaction.
- Rolling a Natural 20 on initiative, Jae Paire begged the party to just walk away, while the GM introduced a new 3D "Final Fantasy Tactics" style camera view in Foundry.
- Melody opened the door, fired her *True Strike* crossbow for 8 damage, and closed the door again.
- Reluctantly, Jae Paire opened the door, stepped within 30 feet of the spirit, and used his Channel Divinity: *Sear Undead*, which the spirit resisted with a 16 on its Wisdom saving throw.
- The undead lunged at Jae Paire, missing both its initial attack and a follow-up reroll fueled by the GM's Fortune Point.
- Alinar entered the dark room and cast *Sacred Flame*, which the undead dodged, before repositioning behind Jae Paire to use the heavily armored cleric as a human shield.
- Aeolus hit the creature with *Starry Wisp* for 10 radiant damage, illuminating it, while his owl familiar Ozzie flew in to grant the Help action.
- Aeolus rolled a 13 on a History check, recognizing the creature as a wraith—specifically Mormesk, a powerful wizard who died centuries ago.
- Melody entered and cast *Vicious Mockery*, insulting the wraith's "heroin chic" bones, though Mormesk succeeded on his saving throw.
- Jae Paire attempted *Sear Undead* a second time, and Mormesk once again succeeded on his DC 15 Wisdom save with a 16.
- Mormesk phased through Jae Paire to attack Alinar for a would-be hit, forcing Alinar to use a charge from his staff to cast *Shield* (raising his AC to 22) and causing the wraith to miss even after spending another GM Fortune Point.
- Alinar retaliated with two beams of *Eldritch Blast*, dealing 20 force damage, bloodying the wraith, and blasting it 20 feet backward.
- Aeolus chipped in 4 radiant damage with *Starry Wisp*, and Melody used her Halfling Luck to turn a Natural 1 into a 15, hitting with her crossbow for 5 magical piercing damage.
- Melody tried to hype up Jae Paire, who used *Sear Undead* a third time; Mormesk succeeded on the save yet again, defying a 1-in-8 probability and prompting a frustrated Jae Paire to walk out of the room while Alinar called him a bitch who was afraid of ghosts.
- Mormesk struck Melody for 23 necrotic damage, though she rolled an exact 14 on her Constitution save to prevent her hit point maximum from being reduced.
- Alinar finished the wraith off with two more *Eldritch Blast* beams for 22 force damage, telling Jae Paire, "This is how you kill a wraith, dirty Frenchman."
- Aeolus rolled a 27 on Arcana, sensing that Mormesk's destruction severed a dark connection in the cave and made the entire complex significantly safer.
- Searching the room and the adjoining wizard quarters, Melody found a scorched chest containing 5 malachite gems, a platinum-filigreed wooden pipe, 1,100 cp, 1,600 sp, and 600 gp, while the party read Mormesk's journals detailing the Smelter Cavern, the Temple of Dumathoin, and the underground lake that creates the cave's echoes.

### The Freshwater Squid Incident

***Aeolus dives headfirst into an underground lake and triggers a frantic battle with a giant freshwater squid.***

- Heading north, the party reached a fork and decided to follow Jae Paire's philosophy of "always go right."
- They entered a cavern featuring a surging underground lake whose waves slamming against the rock created the rhythmic booming heard throughout Wave Echo Cave.
- Overjoyed at seeing water, Aeolus immediately jumped into the lake without looking, coming face-to-face with a giant squid.
- Hoping to avoid a pointless fight, Alinar readied *Witch Bolt* while Jae Paire tossed 5 rations into the water behind the beast, rolling a 19 on Animal Handling to give Aeolus advantage on diplomacy.
- Uninterested in the rations, the giant squid bit Aeolus for 16 damage (rolling a 28 to hit) and lashed out with its tentacles at Jae Paire on the shore, dealing 16 damage, grappling him, and dragging him 10 feet into the water.
- Melody readied a *Bane* spell in case negotiations failed, and Aeolus cast *Speak with Animals*, apologizing for startling the creature and begging it to release Jae Paire.
- Aeolus failed the high-DC Animal Handling check despite having advantage, realizing the creature could not be reasoned with.
- Aeolus immediately activated his *Wrath of the Sea* emanation, blasting the squid 15 feet back and breaking its grapple on Jae Paire, before climbing out of the water.
- Melody's readied *Bane* failed to stick, but Alinar's readied *Witch Bolt* struck the squid for 23 lightning damage, followed immediately by Alinar's turn where he hit with *Eldritch Blast* for 12 force damage and used his bonus action to arc *Witch Bolt* for another 1 damage.
- Jae Paire swam back to shore and successfully cast *Toll the Dead* on the squid, only for the squid to surge forward and grapple him into the water a second time as he spat curses at it.
- Melody cast *Command* ("Drop"), forcing the squid to release Jae Paire once more.
- Aeolus dove gracefully back into the water and used *Wrath of the Sea* again, turning the water around the squid's eyes into a caustic whirlpool for a massive hit.
- Alinar finished the beast off with two *Eldritch Blast* beams and a *Witch Bolt* bonus action, putting calamari on the menu.
- Jae Paire rolled a 19 on Athletics to swim back to shore while Alinar tossed him a spool of platinum cord to help pull him in, and Jae Paire noted with bewilderment that the water was completely fresh, not salt water.
- Alinar used his Magical Cunning feature to recover a Warlock spell slot and cast *Cure Wounds* on Jae Paire, while Aeolus healed himself with his staff.

### Intimidating Bugbears & Silencing the Spider

***Alinar terrifies two bugbear guards into surrendering before the party corners the Spider and opens combat with a Silence spell.***

- Moving north along the stream, Aeolus spotted bugbear guards ahead and used *Message* to inform the party they had likely found where Nundro was being held prisoner.
- Jae Paire and Aeolus convinced a reluctant Alinar to try negotiating rather than launching a surprise attack, with Aeolus casting *Guidance* and providing the Help action.
- Alinar walked up to the bugbears with his hands up, asking about the dwarf, and burned a Luck point to overcome disadvantage before unleashing a furious tirade where he blamed Jae Paire for ruining his day and threatened to murder the bugbears and their families in three seconds.
- Rolling a Natural 20 on Intimidation, Alinar terrified the bugbears so thoroughly that they surrendered immediately without a fight.
- Pressing further in, the party finally came face-to-face with the drow mastermind known as the Spider.
- During a tense and rambling parley, Jae Paire mocked the Spider's name in a Sean Connery voice, questioned his motives regarding Menzoberranzan, and accused him of being an elite creep who tortures children.
- While Alinar held up a finger pretending to confer with the group, Aeolus abruptly ended the conversation by casting *Silence* directly on the Spider, prompting everyone to roll initiative to end the session.

## NPCs

### Mormesk

Mormesk was the last wizard to die defending Wave Echo Cave against a bandit attack centuries ago, and his lingering anger transformed his soul into a hate-filled wraith. He guarded his scorched former quarters and resisted three consecutive *Sear Undead* attempts from Jae Paire before being destroyed by Alinar's *Eldritch Blast*. His journals provided the party with lore regarding the cave's layout and the true acoustic source of its name.

### Giant Squid

A mysteriously adapted freshwater giant squid residing in the underground lake of Wave Echo Cave. After being startled by Aeolus diving into its pool, it rejected Jae Paire's offering of rations and Aeolus's *Speak with Animals* diplomacy, repeatedly grappling Jae Paire into the water before being slain by the party.

### Bugbear Guards

Two bugbears working for the Spider and guarding the area near Nundro Rockseeker. They were completely terrified into submission when Alinar rolled a Natural 20 on Intimidation and threatened to take his frustrations with Jae Paire out on them and their families.

### The Spider

The drow antagonist who has been orchestrating the plot to seize Wave Echo Cave and the Forge of Souls. Cornered by the party, he engaged in a brief, confused conversation with Jae Paire and Alinar before Aeolus enveloped him in a *Silence* spell to kick off combat.

### Nundro Rockseeker

Gundren Rockseeker's brother, who is currently being held prisoner by the Spider's forces in Wave Echo Cave. The party located the area where he is being held but has not yet freed him.

### Ozy

Aeolus's owl familiar. He stood watch during the party's short rest and flew into Mormesk's chamber to provide the Help action during combat.

## Locations

### Wave Echo Cave

The legendary cavern complex housing the Forge of Souls. After the party destroyed the wraith Mormesk, Aeolus sensed that a dark magical connection had been severed, making the entire cave complex significantly safer. The party also discovered the underground freshwater lake whose surging waves create the booming echoes that give the cave its name.

### Forge of Souls Chamber

The room containing the podium for the Forge of Souls. The party took a short rest here, bathed their mundane weapons in the Forge's green fire, and packed the Forge itself into their *Bag of Holding*.

### Mormesk's Quarters

A scorched, darkened room south of the Forge of Souls where the wizards who enchanted weapons at the Forge once lived. It was haunted by the wraith of Mormesk and contained his old journals alongside a scorched treasure chest.
