- **Role & Network:** Known as "The Spider," this drow mage seized Wave Echo Cave to claim the Forge of Souls. He orchestrated regional operations through front factions, including Glasstaff’s Redbrands in Phandalin and King Grol’s Cragmaw raiders.
- **True Objective:** Secretly financed by the Zhentarim, Nezznar sought the Forge's ancient magic to reconstruct fragments of a shattered Netherese obelisk buried beneath Phandalin.
- **Rockseeker Persecution:** His forces murdered Tharden Rockseeker at the cavern's threshold and held Nundro captive under daily interrogation. He also ordered the roadside ambush of Gundren to seize his map to the lost mine.
- **Sanctum Showdown:** Cornered in the Temple of Dumathoin, he was stripped of his spider staff by Aeolus’s _Heat Metal_ and paralyzed by Alinar’s _Hold Person_. Jae Paire then incapacitated the defenseless mage with a non-lethal cudgel blow.
- **Interrogation & Execution:** Under questioning, Nezznar revealed his Zhentarim ties before attempting a failed psychic _Suggestion_ against Alinar. When the drow spat final defiance, Nundro avenged his fallen brother by burying a mining pick in Nezznar's skull.
