# Alinar

Alinar uses his Ioun Stone to create twin scimitars named Sigfried and Roy. He uses these weapons alongside Eldritch Blasts during combat against the Nothic.

## Notes

S42 — Entered a celestial trance to consult Dracalis, learning of Gabriel's scheme to destroy Lathander using the Forge of Souls and Neverwinter Shards.

S43 — Activated his necrotic shroud and executed the fleeing shapeshifting Drow with an Eldritch Blast before directing his attacks at Snarl.

S44 — Revivified Gundren Rockseeker using a 300gp diamond. Intercepted a Grell outside the keep, protected his mind with Intellect Fortress, and received an apocalyptic pre-Celestial message before the creature exploded.
