# The Story So Far...

What started as a local borderland scrap with mountain bandits and dragons spiraled into divine heresy and cosmic dread across the Sword Coast. **[[The Raging Song]]** proved their grit on the crags of Icespire Peak by slaying the white dragon [[Cryovain]]—until they pulled silver ichor from the beast's skull, confirming that mind flayer parasites were pulling the strings behind the chaos. 

When ancient fey pacts claimed their friend [[Drakka]] to the timeless wilds of the Feywild, the party had zero time to process before **[[Jae Paire]]** crashed onto the scene. Armed with a French accent, endless optimism, long-winded speeches, and weaponized "power of friendship."

## A Tale of Two Dragons

As shadows stretched over [[Neverwinter Wood]], every member of the crew hit their limits across crumbling keeps and ruined towns. **[[Aeolus Stormcaller]]**, the pragmatic sea elf druid, grounded the party's wild momentum—summoning jagged arcs of lightning, slamming foes into dungeon walls with caustic ocean storms, and plunging into uncharted underground lakes. **[[Melody Sparkleheart]]**, the halfling bard, turned combat into pure theater. She sent monsters running with a banshee-like mantle of majesty, sniped horrors through their central pupils, and endured a bitter betrayal after learning her shady ex-manager, [[Ziggy]], was bankrolling cultists in Thundertree to seize ancient forges for cash. In those overgrown ruins, the party uncovered a living illithid tadpole before driving off [[Venomfang]]. The green dragon appeared to be in cahoots with the resident cultists of the abandoned town.

## Forging Ahead

All those threads pulled the adventurers into the echoing halls of Wave Echo Cave, where the celestial warlock **[[Alinar Dormitor]]** took center stage. Balancing dry wit and lawyer-grade quips against bursts of celestial fury, Alinar flared with necrotic wings and lightless eyes—blasting horrors with crackling beams of moonlight and sunlight while defying psychic assaults through sheer stubborn grit. 

After locating the legendary [[Forge of Souls]] and prying its mystical brazier from its pedestal to stash inside his _Bag of Holding_, Alinar and the crew cornered [[Nezznar, the Spider]] beside the shrine of Dumathoin. With Aeolus smothering the chamber in silence, Melody breaking the drow's guards with debilitating song, Jae delivering a non-lethal cudgel bonk, and Alinar terrifying the mastermind into a full confession, the drow plotter met his end under [[Nundro Rockseeker]]'s vengeful mining pick.

Yet, as the bloodied heroes gather their loot and step toward the sunlight, their victory in [[Wave Echo Cave]] leaves as many questions as resolutions. Tucked inside Alinar's dimensional pack sits the Forge of Souls.

Meanwhile, Ziggy and the [[Zhentarim]] are pouring fortunes into new _Magic Mouth_ audio boxes. Venomfang is likely licking his wounds somewhere beyond the Neverwinter Wood. And the illithid threat remains a mystery. Are all of these connected to the Netherese shards in the party's possession?
