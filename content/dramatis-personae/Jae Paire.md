# Jae Paire

Jae Paire can transform into a suave persona to intimidate targets, causing them to act with disadvantage. In truth, he comes off as a bit of a well-intentioned nitwit. He also uses sounds, like an invisible bell toll, for effect. He is accompanied by spirit guardians that manifest as tiny French versions of himself who fight using bones and a skull.
