# Aeolus

Aeolus is a party member who encountered three bodies at the bottom of a cavern, including two grotesquely malformed goblins with unnatural claws.

## Notes

S43 — Used Pass without Trace and his osprey familiar Ozzy to scout Cragmaw Castle, wildshaped into a spider to inspect King Groll's room, and cast Lightning Bolt in combat.

S44 — Withstood a critical blow from King Groll before incinerating him with a point-blank Lightning Bolt, and helped attack the fleeing Grell outside.
