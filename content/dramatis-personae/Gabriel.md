# Story

A solar who devoted himself to [[Amaunator]] when the [[spell plague]] caused Lathander's disappearance. According to [[Dracalis]], he is now obsessed with perfect order and willing to make bargains outside of Amaunator's domain. [[Alinar Dormitor|Alinar]] prides himself on being an age long thorn in Gabriel's side.
