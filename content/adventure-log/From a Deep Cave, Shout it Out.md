# Summary

Deep within the subterranean reaches of [[Wave Echo Cave]], the adventurers navigated a realm bathed in eerie, phosphorescent waves of ancient magic. In an abandoned campsite near a sheer twenty-foot chasm, [[Jae Paire]] and [[Alinar Dormitor|Alinar]] uncovered the tragic remains of [[The Rockseekers|Tharden]] Rockseeker, one of the three dwarven brothers whose expedition had met a grim end. Seeking answers, Jae Paire channeled the divine power of [[Ilmater]] to cast *Speak with Dead* upon Tharden's corpse. The slain dwarf animated stiffly, revealing that he had been impaled by rockfall and finished off by the mysterious forces of [[Nezznar the Spider|The Spider]]—a sinister drow mage allied with savage bugbears. Before slipping back into eternal rest, Tharden imparted a characteristically blunt farewell for his surviving brothers: Nundro was an idiot, Gundren was a butthead, and he would miss them dearly. 

After retrieving an enchanted cloak from the fallen dwarf and identifying it as a *Cloak of Protection*, the party prepared to delve deeper into the lower tier of the cavern. One by one, they scaled down a twenty-foot rope into the chasm floor, passing the unsettling, elongated skull of a mutated goblin corpse. Alinar descended with celestial elegance on fluttering spectral wings, immediately detecting a suffocating blanket of necrotic energy saturating the stonework. Unsure of which tunnel to pursue, Alinar rolled a set of fortune tokens purchased from Toff to gamble on their navigational destiny, pointing them toward the eastern corridors littered with the desiccated, rusted remains of ancient dwarven and human miners. However, tactical stealth disintegrated into immediate catastrophe when Jae Paire bellowed across the echoing stone halls to ask Alinar what he was doing, alerting the denizens of the deep cavern.

The party’s shouts awakened the slumbering horrors of the complex. Distant, resonant bellows shook the stone as three ravenous swarms of blood-drinking stirges descended from above, closely followed by a shambling vanguard of undead, including two gargantuan ogre zombies. Jae Paire’s initial protective aura was broken when stirges swarmed and grappled him, draining his vitals. Refusing to yield, Jae fell back on ancient tactical doctrine, establishing a veritable choke point at the mouth of the corridor by conjuring a glowing *Guardian of Faith*. [[Melody Sparkleheart|Melody]] altered the flow of time itself by blanketing the oncoming undead horde in a crippling *Slow* spell, severely slashing their defenses and movement speed. Exploiting the narrow bottleneck, Aeolus unleashed a crackling *Lightning Bolt* directly down the hallway, incinerating multiple zombies instantly, before whipping the stirges with surging tidal waves from *Wrath of the Sea*.

The battle devolved into a relentless meat grinder within the corridor. Alinar blasted stirges off his comrades with repelling crackles of *Eldritch Blast*, capping off the rescue by delivering a firm "dope slap of healing" to Jae Paire’s helmet via *Healing Light*. The lumbering ogre zombies were shredded upon entering the range of the *Guardian of Faith*, which sheared massive chunks of necrotic flesh from the hulking monstrosities before fading. Jae Paire charged in with his battleaxe to cleave into the surviving brute, and Aeolus delivered the gory coup de grâce: channeling a concentrated jet of briny sea water directly into the exposed, gaping chest cavity of the ogre zombie, causing the behemoth to violently burst from the inside out in a shower of putrid viscera.

With the shambling remnants mopped up, the party paused in an ancient intersection beneath thirty-five-foot-high ceilings etched with welcoming reliefs of dwarf and gnome miners. Jae Paire’s keen eye deduced that a collapsed passage to the south represented the original grand entrance to Wave Echo Cave. Seeking guidance on how to reach the Forge of Souls, Alinar reached across planes to communed with his celestial ally, [[Dracalis]]. The entity warned that [[Gabriel]]'s machinations threatened the forge, urging haste and nudging the party eastward through the fungal tunnels. Venturing toward the mycelial expanse, Jae attempted to commune spiritually with the subterranean flora—discovering only that they were probably not edible—before Aeolus stepped forward and narrowly dodged an explosive spore burst, leaving the party on edge as new cavern hazards loomed ahead.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Echoes from Beyond the Grave
#### *The adventurers discover a campsite in Wave Echo Cave and awaken a dead dwarf to extract vital intelligence.*
- Jae Paire and Alinar thoroughly investigate the campsite at the chasm's rim, discovering the body of Tharden Rockseeker, who succumbed to untended injuries from stonefall.
- Jae Paire casts *Speak with Dead* on Tharden to interrogate the corpse regarding the Forge of Souls, cavern dangers, and the Spider.
- Tharden reveals that the Spider is a drow accompanied by bugbears, and leaves insulting yet fond final messages for his brothers Gundren and Nundro.
- Alinar casts *Identify* as a ritual on Tharden’s garment, revealing it to be a magical *Cloak of Protection*.

### Scene 2: Descent into the Chasm
#### *The party navigates the descent into the lower cavern tier and gambles on their directional fate.*
- Melody climbs twenty feet down a fixed rope into the chasm (Athletics check 14), discovering the bizarre corpse of a goblin with an elongated, cone-shaped skull.
- Aeolus casts *Guidance* and scales down the rope, taking a point of scuffing damage after slipping on an 11 check.
- Jae Paire spends a Heroic Inspiration to score a dirty 20 Athletics roll, landing with warrior-like grace.
- Alinar rolls a Natural 20 on Acrobatics, his spectral aasimar wings flaring as he senses heavy necrotic energy permeating the lower caverns.
- Alinar rolls Toff's fate tokens (rolling two moons and one bone) to determine whether the party heads right or left.
- Alinar inspects ancient skeletal remains of dwarves and humans whose rusted gear disintegrates to ash.

### Scene 3: The Bottleneck at the Hot Gates
#### *A loud blunder echoes across the caves, pulling swarms of stirges and a horde of zombies into a deadly choke point.*
- Jae Paire shouts across the caverns to Alinar, completely blowing the party's stealth and waking up swarms of stirges and zombies.
- The table pauses for a quick five-minute scotch break to brace for the inevitable tactical fallout.
- Three swarms of stirges swoop into melee; Jae Paire casts *Spirit Guardians* and moves up, while Aeolus activates *Wrath of the Sea*.
- Melody casts a clutch *Slow* spell, ensnaring two ogre zombies and several humanoid zombies, reducing their AC and movement speed by half.
- Stirges swarm Jae Paire, dealing piercing damage and breaking his concentration on *Spirit Guardians*, while another swarm grapples Aeolus.
- Aeolus utilizes *Wrath of the Sea* to blast the stirges off himself, then threads a pinpoint *Lightning Bolt* through the hallway, annihilating three zombies and heavily wounding two ogre zombies.
- Jae Paire deploys his *Guardian of Faith* at the corridor bottleneck, dealing 20 radiant damage automatically to advancing enemies.
- Alinar picks off stirges with *Eldritch Blast* and restores Jae Paire's vitality using a "dope slap of healing" via *Healing Light*.
- Jae Paire strikes an ogre zombie with his battleaxe and heals himself with *Healing Word*.
- Aeolus pumps a high-pressure stream of briny sea water into the wounded ogre zombie's open wounds, detonating it from the inside out; the remaining slowed zombies are easily mopped up as combat ends.

### Scene 4: Reliefs, Dracalis, and Toxic Spores
#### *The party uncovers the original entrance to the mines and seeks celestial guidance before stepping into a fungal trap.*
- Jae Paire rolls a 20 on History to inspect ancient carved reliefs depicting dwarf and gnome miners, deducing that a collapsed southern passage was the grand original entrance to Wave Echo Cave.
- Aeolus detects a corrupted mycelial network to the east; Melody feels instincts pulling her forward toward the Forge of Souls.
- Alinar rolls a 21 Arcana check to reach Dracalis through the magical interference; Dracalis warns that Gabriel's plot threatens the Forge of Souls and points east as the lesser of evils.
- The party ventures east; Jae Paire rolls a failed Survival check at disadvantage attempting to commune spiritually with the cavern mushrooms, concluding only that they shouldn't be eaten.
- Aeolus walks through the fungi and succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw to evade a sudden burst of explosive spores as the session ends.

## NPCs

### Tharden Rockseeker
One of the three Rockseeker brothers who sought Wave Echo Cave. He perished alone in his camp from untended stonefall wounds suffered on the journey, having been stripped of his valuables except for his cloak. He was briefly reanimated via *Speak with Dead* by Jae Paire, providing crucial reconnaissance on the Spider and calling Gundren a butthead.

### Dracalis
Alinar’s celestial guide and patron contact. Reached via planar telepathy despite the heavy magical interference within Wave Echo Cave, Dracalis urged the party to secure the Forge of Souls immediately to hinder Gabriel’s catastrophic plot against Lathander.

## Locations

### Wave Echo Cave — Chasm Campsite
A rugged campsite established on the upper ledge of a twenty-foot-deep chasm within Wave Echo Cave. It contained the abandoned bedrolls and body of Tharden Rockseeker, as well as an ancient rope descending into the lower depths.

### The Miner's Crossroads
A thirty-five-foot-high subterranean intersection decorated with ancient decorative stone reliefs honoring dwarf and gnome miners. The southern passage represents the grand, albeit completely caved-in, original entrance to the mine complex.

### The Eastern Fungal Cavern
A humid, subterranean cavern choked with a vast mycelial network of strange, glowing fungi. The area radiates corruption and volatile spore hazards that trigger when stepped on.

## Items

### Cloak of Protection
A shimmering, enchanted dwarven cloak stripped from the corpse of Tharden Rockseeker. Identified via a ritual cast by Alinar, it grants a +1 bonus to Armor Class and saving throws, requiring attunement. It was given to Aeolus to attune to during their next rest.

### Amulet of Health
A magic talisman previously carried by the party that sets the wearer's Constitution score to 19. It was retroactively attuned to Jae Paire to bolster his durability and hit point pool to 51.

### Toff's Marked Tokens
A set of fortune-telling tokens depicting bones, suns, and moons, purchased by Alinar. Rolled to resolve a directional dilemma, producing two moons and directing the party toward the eastern tunnel.

## Spells

### Speak with Dead
Cast by Jae Paire as a 3rd-level spell targeting the corpse of Tharden Rockseeker, allowing the party to ask five questions regarding cavern layout, his death, and the Spider.

### Spirit Guardians
Cast by Jae Paire at 3rd level, manifesting an aura of spectral guardians. It was prematurely disrupted when a swarm of stirges attached to Jae and broke his concentration.

### Guardian of Faith
Summoned by Jae Paire via his spirit necklace at the hallway bottleneck. The spectral sentinel effectively anchored the party's choke point, dealing 20 radiant damage to incoming enemies before exhausting its 60-damage capacity.

### Slow
A 3rd-level transmutation spell cast by Melody covering an expansive area of the undead horde. It halved the targets' movement speed, imposed a -2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saves, and eliminated extra actions for half the encounter's combatants.

### Lightning Bolt
Cast by Aeolus down the narrow cavern corridor. The bolt dealt heavy lightning damage across a straight line, instantly killing three zombies and severely wounding two ogre zombies.

### Eldritch Blast
Cast repeatedly by Alinar with Repelling Blast. Used to pick off stirges and blast a blood-drinking swarm off Jae Paire while pushing targets back 10 feet.

### Healing Light
A celestial class resource expended by Alinar to restore 9 hit points to Jae Paire, flavored as a stern "dope slap of healing" delivered to the back of Jae's helmet.
